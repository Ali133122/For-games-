LANTERN DRIFT

Your lantern is going out. Gather embers to keep it burning. Wraiths creep in from the dark, and each touch drains your light. Stranger creatures emerge the longer you last.

Move: WASD / arrow keys / mouse, or drag on touch screens
Flare: Space or click. Spend light to burn away nearby wraiths.
Sound: M to mute

Power-ups appear now and then. Grab them before they fade:
Ward blocks one hit · Swift speed boost · Stillness freezes wraiths · Lure pulls in embers
